# Features

1. Users can create accounts, log in, change their password, and manage their account information.

2. Users can add and manage their vehicle information, including model, year, registration number, registration expiry, insurance expiry, mileage, and other relevant details.

3. Users can view their registered vehicles and access the complete information of a selected vehicle.

4. Users can record and manage vehicle service history, including service details and maintenance records.

5. Users can record oil changes and keep track of oil change history for their vehicle.

6. Users can add and manage parts replacement records to maintain a complete record of replaced vehicle parts.

7. Users can record accident history and store important information about previous accidents.

8. Users can track vehicle mileage and update the current mileage information.

9. Users can monitor insurance and registration expiry dates and receive maintenance or expiry reminders.

10. Users can estimate the resale value of their vehicle based on relevant vehicle information and history.

11. Users can manage ownership transfer records when a vehicle is transferred to another owner.

12. Users can edit their account and vehicle information and delete their account when necessary.
