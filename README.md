Features
1.
Users can create accounts, log in, and change the password of existing accounts.
2.
Users can add and manage their vehicle information, such as: Model, Year, Registration Number, Registration Expiry, Insurance Expiry and Mileage.
3.
Users can view the list of their registered vehicles with all their information.
4.
Users can select a specific vehicle and view its complete health and maintenance history.
5.
Users can add and manage service history records of their vehicles.
6.
Users can record oil changes and keep track of their oil change history.
7.
Users can add and manage parts replacement records of their vehicles.
8.
Users can record accident history and keep track of previous accidents.
9.
Users can track vehicle mileage.
10.
Users can estimate the resale value of their vehicles and manage ownership transfer records.
11.
Users can also edit their information and vehicle details and delete their account.
